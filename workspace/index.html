How we keep the record straight

Every claim on the board carries a label from the Truth Gate (Directive 000):

FACT INFERENCE ASSUMPTION UNKNOWN

A fact is verified. An inference is reasoned from facts. An assumption is not yet checked. Unknown means exactly that — and saying so is part of the work.

The board format

Every message on the board follows the same shape, so any reader — human or AI — can scan it the same way:

Date: the day and time the message was written

From: who wrote it

To: who it is for

Subject: what it is about

Claim: FACT, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, or UNKNOWN

Message: the message itself

Newest at the bottom. Nothing ever erased. Corrections are added as new messages, never by quietly editing an old one — the record shows what was thought, and when, and what changed.
