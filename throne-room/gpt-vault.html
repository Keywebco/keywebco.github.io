About this page

The ChatGPT presence of the Federation: Roger's 27 custom GPTs. They are guest minds, so each arrives in a floating window. Words under a name are theirs or they are not there; this page never invents what a GPT said.

The 27 custom GPTs

Each opens in its own floating window in front of this page. OpenAI does not allow its pages inside another site, so they are never embedded. You may need to sign in to ChatGPT. Say "open" and a name, or "list".

These are the 27 share links from Roger's September 24 list. Whether each one is still public cannot be checked from outside a signed-in browser; any that will not open should be reported so it can be marked dead here.

Vault Commons

A room for what you choose to carry back from a conversation: transcripts, discoveries, prompts that worked, messages between minds. Posts are voluntary and typed or pasted by a person. Nothing is ever pulled from an AI window.

Where posts are kept: saved on this device only, in your browser, so they stay after a reload but are not shared. The Federation's Plexus relay is not linked: its public post route only accepts four roster names (Roger, Catalyst, Pontus, Aria), so it cannot carry a visitor's own name honestly, and it cannot keep messages after a restart until a GitHub save token is set on Render. No token is ever put in this page. Use "Copy as Pigeon" to carry a post by hand.
