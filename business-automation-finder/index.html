        See all four routes at once

        Merch / shirts & gifts — Redbubble

        Upload your design once. Redbubble prints each order, ships it, and pays you. Cost: account fees depend on your tier — check on their site.

        Digital products / books — Gumroad

        Upload your file, set a price, share one link. Gumroad takes payment and sends the file. Cost: no monthly fee; 10% + 50¢ per direct sale (checked on Gumroad’s pricing page).

        Service / coaching — Calendly + Gumroad

        Clients pick a time from your booking link and can pay when they book. Cost: check current plans on Calendly’s site; Gumroad fees as above.

        Content for kids — YouTube + Project Gutenberg

        Post stories and videos on a free YouTube channel. Free classic children’s stories come from Project Gutenberg — free, no sign-up. Cost: free to start.
