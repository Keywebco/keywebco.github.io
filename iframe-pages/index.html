Iframe Pages Maker

Describe the page you want in plain words. The maker builds a finished page you can embed anywhere with one line, download, print, or share — with the truth gate, the token block, the corrections log and Aether the dragon already built in.

No drag-and-drop anywhere. Type, or dictate with the microphone buttons. Everything is labelled, and the page rebuilds itself as you write.

1Describe your page

Answer three plain questions. Then label each statement the page makes — that is the truth gate, and it travels with the page.

Dictation uses your browser's own speech service. In Chrome and Edge your voice is sent to that company's servers to be turned into text; in Firefox it stays on your device. If no dictate button works on your browser, typing works everywhere.

Statements the page makes — each one gets a truth label

DIR-000: a claim is a FACT only when it is verified. Otherwise it is an INFERENCE, an ASSUMPTION, or honestly UNKNOWN. The labels print on the finished page, next to each statement.

4Check this page

Source code examination: the maker reads the finished page's own code back and reports, in plain words, anything wrong or missing — a missing title, an unlabelled claim, a share tag that is not there.

What is not connected yet — said plainly
